- External integration: api-integration, testing-and-quality, debugging.
- Smart-home integration: home-automation, mcp-server-development, security-engineering.
- Frontend platform: frontend-engineering, design-systems, performance-optimization.
- Production release: code-review, devops-and-deployment, security-engineering, documentation.
